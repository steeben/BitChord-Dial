Dial 1.5.0

### Important: Dial is now a new app
- Dial's package name is now com.steeben.dial (it was com.steeben.bitchord), so this version installs as a separate app beside your current one instead of updating it. Before uninstalling the old app, use Export data in its settings and Import data in the new one to bring over your settings, playlists, dial pins and listening history, then sign in again.
- Downloads now save to Music/Dial instead of Music/BitChord. Songs already in Music/BitChord stay where they are: move them into Music/Dial to keep them in your library, or delete the old folder.
