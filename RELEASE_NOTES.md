Dial 1.5.0

### Important: Dial is now a new app
- Dial's package name is now com.steeben.dial (it was com.steeben.bitchord), so this version installs as a separate app beside your current one instead of updating it. Your data and sign-ins don't carry over on their own: the new app shows a notice with a Take Me There button the first time it opens. Before uninstalling the old app, use Export data in its settings (Your data) and Import data in the new one to bring over your settings, playlists, dial pins and listening history, then sign in again.
- Downloads now save to Music/Dial instead of Music/BitChord. Songs already in Music/BitChord stay where they are: move them into Music/Dial to keep them in your library, or delete the old folder.


### Player
- The mini player becomes the full player and shrinks back into it, with the bar sliding away underneath; a pull that falls short lands the cover back where it belongs
- The playhead glow rides along the mini player and the player panel's edge, and lights up for the restored track too
- The mini player's cover never flashes the placeholder, and the cover's shadow arrives with the first picture
- One accent colour per song, used everywhere, with light text on bright, colourful art; the ambient blobs are back to their old look
- Music videos never play on their own: they play audio only with the thumbnail, and videos that are a single still picture fall back to the backdrop
- Landscape player: tap empty space for the full-screen video, tap the video to come back

### Playlists, releases and artists
- Playlist pages get a generated collage image, a backdrop on every playlist, and Play, Shuffle and Search buttons
- Custom cover dialog with a cover generator you can ask to pick again, and a custom cover can be the backdrop too
- Release and artist pages: the name docks in the top bar as you scroll, a round Search between Shuffle and Play opens out to the full width, and the ⋯ menus grow out of the bar's pill
- Artist pages get Shuffle, Play and Search like a playlist, with star, radio and ⋯ in the top bar
- Search always lands at the top and glides back down when you leave it

### Home and dial
- Tapping the Home tab on Home returns to the top and the dial's first page
- Dial tiles pop in once per deal instead of on every page swipe, and the die stays put through a pull

### Settings
- Account & integrations and Listen together slide in as pages, and Search's field is in liquid glass
- The app says Dial everywhere instead of BitChord
