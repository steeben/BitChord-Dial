BitChord Dial 1.3.0

### Player
- Play/pause morphs between its shapes, buttons pulse a soft glow when pressed, and the ⋯ menu grows out of its button
- Liking a song pops the heart red with a burst of lines before it settles back to monochrome
- Predictive back out of the lyrics, the queue and Settings; the queue's sleeve follows it like a pull-down over the controls
- Music videos play as a full-screen Canvas on an upright phone, cropped behind the controls; a video with no song version keeps its own audio and drops only the picture
- Tapping a music video plays its song from the top, and a song that replaced a video can keep that video as its Canvas
- The video Canvas stays up coming back from the queue or lyrics and across a rotation
- New "Fix mismatched Canvas/audio" in the player's menu: wrong audio, wrong clip, wrong video, out of sync, or undo, saved per song
- The audio version of a song plays by default, and AutoPlay follows it when it's swapped in
- Apple-style 3:4 motion covers under the whole screen
- Shuffle sits in the middle of the queue's mode capsule; Repeat all and AutoPlay exclude each other
- Player drawers slide away on back and wear liquid glass; a setting hides the output / Listen Together pill
- Canvas clips sweep in and out on a diagonal, with each clip on its own player so hand-offs don't flash
- Scrolling titles fade softly at their ends instead of cutting off
- Sleep timer with a custom duration, and playback pauses at zero volume and resumes when it comes back

### Mixing and audio
- Automix: a phrase-based engine with a mix view and loop mix
- AI Mix plays the queue as a DJ set of samples up to 50 seconds each, starting mid-song, and is in album menus
- Quality upgrades blend the better copy in over two seconds instead of cutting, and splice hi-res in when the output can carry it
- Direct Source Stream as a choice beside the mid-song upgrade; lossless answers are taken without waiting out every source
- 24-bit FLAC stays intact on hi-res Bluetooth, and ALAC decodes through FFmpeg
- 30-second previews served as full tracks are skipped, and live stations keep their window moving

### Spotify
- An optional toggle mirrors your likes onto Spotify Liked Songs
- A Spotify tab in Search plays hits through YouTube Music
- Spotify playlists can be pinned to the speed dial

### Home
- Explore is now For You, laid out like official BitChord with Dial's mark
- The speed dial leads with this device's own plays, with recents after the pins and ahead of YouTube's dial
- Top Picks for You under the dials, and a play refreshes only Recents
- Pinned dial tiles lift into their menu

### Look and feel
- iOS-style pop-up menus on liquid glass, Settings with a page per category, back chevrons and frosted-glass switches
- Liquid Glass options for transparency, tint, darkness, saturation and blur, matched across the mini player and nav bar
- A Dark app icon, and BitChord's own icons (Neon among them) after Dial's
- Menus stay portrait on phones while the player still rotates
- A rebuilt Settings footer around the app's name

### Everywhere else
- Korean translation, and new strings translated into every app language
- Podcasts open with their full episode list and play
- Hide user info for clean screenshots, and Remove from history on the History screen
- Lyrics use Vazirmatn for Persian; song matching handles CJK translations and pt-BR credits
- Fixes for casting downloaded tracks, Listen Together back navigation, slow addons and a stuck "Upgrading" badge

Installs over the previous build of BitChord Dial and keeps your sign-in.
