Dial 1.7.1

### Player buttons
- In the Glass style, the Like button, the ⋯ button and the bottom pill use a fixed glass rule.
- The rule is: transparency 100%, blur 10dp, saturation 100%.
- The Liquid Glass options in Settings do not change these buttons.
- The buttons do not have a tint. Dark symbols are easier to see on bright covers.
- The Frost style and the Solid style do not change.

### Progress bar
- This change applies only to the glow progress bar.
- When the cover is bright, the played part of the bar is dark. It has the same colour as the player text.
- Near the playhead, the colour changes from dark to the accent colour, and then to white.
- The light at the playhead is white.
- When the cover is dark, the bar does not change.
